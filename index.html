Our story

One family.
One long table.

Giuseppe Rossi was born just outside Naples, where Sunday meant a crowded kitchen, a pot that had been simmering since morning, and his grandmother's hands teaching him what no recipe could. In 1978, he brought those flavours to a little neighbourhood corner and opened Osteria Rosso.

Four decades later, the room is fuller, but the welcome is the same. Nonno still arrives before the sun, still rolls the pasta by hand, and still tastes every sauce before it leaves the kitchen.

“Good food fills the belly. Food made with love fills the heart.”
